Appearance

App lock

Hides your notes behind a PIN on this device. It does not encrypt the stored notes.

Stylus / Bluetooth pen

Drawing supports Pointer Events, including compatible Apple Pencil and active styluses. Pressure sensitivity is used when the browser exposes it. Websites cannot universally detect every Bluetooth pen by its Bluetooth connection: iPadOS Safari does not support Web Bluetooth, and many pens communicate directly with the tablet instead of exposing a browser-visible Bluetooth service.

Tip: open Sketch pad and draw with your pen. If the browser reports the input as a pen, the status will update. Bluetooth pairing alone cannot guarantee stylus support.

Data & backup

Notes save to this browser first. Export a JSON backup regularly. Browser storage may be erased if you clear site data.
